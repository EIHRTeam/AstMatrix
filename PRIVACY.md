# AstMatrix Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 23, 2026**

AstMatrix is a QR code and barcode generator plugin for Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop and Figma, developed by the Endfield Industries Human Resource Team ("EIHRTeam", "we" or "us").

## Summary

AstMatrix runs entirely on your own device. It does not collect, upload or sell any data, contains no online services, and includes no telemetry, analytics or crash reporting of any kind.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

AstMatrix does not collect, store, transmit or share any information, including but not limited to:

- Personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, account details or device identifiers);
- The content you enter or encode into a QR code or barcode;
- Your design files, layers, artboards or any other document content;
- Usage data, such as feature clicks, session length or usage patterns;
- Crash reports, error logs or diagnostic information.

## 2. How Your Data Is Processed Locally

The content you enter into the plugin (for example a URL, text or a product number) is used only to generate a QR code or barcode graphic on your device. The result is inserted into the Illustrator, Photoshop or Figma document you currently have open.

All of this happens locally. The content never leaves your device and is never sent to EIHRTeam or to any third party.

## 3. No Online Services, No Telemetry

AstMatrix does not rely on any online service and does not send data to EIHRTeam or to any third-party server, including but not limited to:

- Usage statistics and behavioral analytics;
- Crash and error reporting;
- Account sign-in, cloud sync or license verification;
- Advertising or third-party tracking components.

## 4. Host Applications and Distribution Platforms

AstMatrix is offered in versions for Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop and Figma, and may be distributed through Adobe Exchange, Figma Community or GitHub Releases. These applications and platforms are operated by their respective providers, who may collect and process data under their own privacy policies (for example download counts, installation information or document synchronization). That processing is outside our control and outside the scope of this policy. Please refer to:

- Adobe Privacy Policy: <https://www.adobe.com/privacy.html>
- Figma Privacy Policy: <https://www.figma.com/legal/privacy/>
- GitHub Privacy Statement: <https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement>

Please note that the QR codes and barcodes generated by the plugin become part of your design document. If you save, export or share that document, or use cloud collaboration features (for example by sharing a Figma file), its contents are handled according to the rules of the host application.

## 5. Open Source and Verifiability

AstMatrix is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/EIHRTeam/AstMatrix/blob/main/LICENSE), and its source code is publicly available at <https://github.com/EIHRTeam/AstMatrix>. You can review the code at any time to verify the statements in this policy.

## 6. Children's Privacy

AstMatrix does not collect personal information from any user, and therefore does not collect personal information from children.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this document and the effective date above, and previous versions remain available in the repository's commit history. If a future version introduces any feature that collects data or connects to the network, we will update this policy before that version is released and state the change clearly in the release notes.
